## Companies on both lists

Only these five show up in both the top funding tier and the top research tier:

- **OpenAI** — heavy funding, frontier LLM research, 500M+ weekly ChatGPT users
- **Anthropic** — heavy funding, safety-focused research, ~$3B ARR, 1,000%+ YoY growth
- **Google AI** — Alphabet's balance sheet, largest research org on either list
- **Nvidia AI** — funding momentum tied to hardware demand, elite systems research
- **Microsoft AI** — Microsoft's capital base, research-first org structure since 2024
## Why the overlap matters

Sustained AI research is expensive and slow to pay off. Companies that can't raise capital at scale eventually can't sustain research teams large enough to stay technically relevant. Conversely, funding without research depth tends to produce a company that executes one narrow product well (hiring, support automation, no-code tooling) rather than one setting technical direction for the field.
